Dengue is a viral infection spread to people through the bite of infected Aedes mosquitoes.

There is no specific treatment for dengue. Early detection and access to proper medical care lower the risk of severe illness.

People with symptoms such as high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, muscle and joint pain, nausea or rash should consult a doctor.

Removing standing water around the home helps prevent mosquito breeding.
