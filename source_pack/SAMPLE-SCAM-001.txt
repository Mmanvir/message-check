Banks and government departments never ask customers to share their OTP, PIN or password over phone calls, SMS or messaging apps.

Messages that ask you to click a link to claim a prize, refund or subsidy, or to avoid your bank account being blocked, are a common fraud method.

Report suspected cyber fraud on the national cyber crime helpline 1930.
